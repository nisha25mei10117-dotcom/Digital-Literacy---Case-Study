# Digital-Literacy---Case-Study
# Introduction
Digital literacy refers to the ability to effectively and responsibly use digital tools and technologies. In today’s academic and professional environment, students rely heavily on online platforms for learning, communication, and collaboration. This project explores how digital tools are used in everyday academic life and highlights the importance of awareness while using them.

# Importance of Digital Literacy
Digital literacy is essential for students as it helps them communicate effectively, protect their personal information, and use technology responsibly. It enables students to identify online risks such as phishing and cyber fraud. It also supports academic growth by allowing students to use tools like coding platforms, design tools, and collaboration platforms efficiently. Without proper awareness, students may misuse technology or become vulnerable to cyber threats.

# Case Study Example
A student received an email offering a paid internship from a reputed company. The message appeared professional and created urgency to respond quickly. Without verifying the sender, the student clicked on the link and entered personal details. Later, suspicious activity was detected in the student’s account. It was found to be a phishing attack. This case shows how lack of verification and quick responses can lead to serious consequences.

# Conclusion
This project highlights that digital literacy is not just about using tools but also about making responsible decisions. Students must develop habits such as verifying information, maintaining professional communication, and being cautious online. By combining technical skills with awareness, students can use digital platforms safely and effectively for their academic and professional growth.
